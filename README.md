## 1. Bismillah — Opening Note

> *"And say: My Lord, increase me in knowledge."*
> — Surah Taha (20:114)

**Assalamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuh.**

IMIN was born from a simple observation: Indian Muslim creators who wish to stay true to their deen, their ethics, and their creativity have no dedicated home. No directory. No collective. No safe workspace where the rules of halal content creation are not the exception — but the foundation.

This repository is that home. It is open-source because knowledge in Islam was never meant to be hoarded. The scholars of the Golden Age — Ibn Sina, Al-Khwarizmi, Ibn al-Haytham — built upon each other's work freely. We carry that torch.

> *"The best of people are those who are most beneficial to people."*
> — Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (Al-Mu'jam Al-Awsat, Tabarani)

This project is a sadaqah jariyah — an ongoing charity of effort, code, and community.

## 2. What is IMIN?

**IMIN (Indian Muslims Influencer Network)** is an open-source initiative that provides:

| Pillar | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 📂 **Directory** | A curated, searchable registry of Indian Muslim influencers who create ethical, faceless, music-free content |
| 🛠️ **Workspace** | A collaborative digital space for ideation, project planning, asset sharing, and peer feedback |
| 🤝 **Network** | A brotherhood/sisterhood of like-minded creators who uplift each other without competition |
| 📚 **Knowledge Base** | Shared resources on ethical monetisation, Islamic content guidelines, and creative techniques |
| 🚀 **Launchpad** | A springboard for collaborative campaigns, halal brand partnerships, and community projects |

IMIN is **not** a talent agency. It is **not** a monetisation platform. It is a **collective** — a digital majlis where Indian Muslim creators gather, create, and grow together.

## 3. Our Islamic Foundation

Every aspect of IMIN is anchored in Quran and Sunnah. This is not decorative — it is structural.

### 3.1 Intention (Niyyah)

> *"Actions are judged by intentions, and every person shall have what they intended."*
> — Sahih al-Bukhari (1), Sahih Muslim (1907)

Every contributor joins with a stated niyyah. This is not performative. It is a reminder that our work here is an act of ibadah if done sincerely.

### 3.2 Excellence (Ihsan)

> *"Indeed, Allah has prescribed Ihsan (excellence) in everything."*
> — Sahih Muslim (1955)

We do not produce mediocre content because we are "just halal." We produce *exceptional* content that also happens to be halal. The constraint elevates the craft.

### 3.3 Brotherhood & Sisterhood (Ukhuwwah)

> *"The believers are but brothers."*
> — Surah Al-Hujurat (49:10)

IMIN rejects the toxic competition of mainstream influencer culture. Here, your growth is my growth. Your success is celebrated, not envied.

### 3.4 Truthfulness (Sidq)

> *"O you who believe! Fear Allah and be with those who are true."*
> — Surah At-Tawbah (9:119)

No fake engagement. No inflated metrics. No deceptive sponsorships. What you see in this directory is real.

### 3.5 Trust (Amanah)

> *"Indeed, Allah commands you to render trusts to whom they are due."*
> — Surah An-Nisa (4:58)

Community ideas, shared assets, and collaborative IP are treated as amanah. Violating this trust is grounds for removal.

### 3.6 Avoiding Riya (Showing Off)

The faceless, anonymous-by-design nature of IMIN content is a safeguard against riya. When the audience cannot see *you*, the work speaks for the message, not the ego.

## 4. The Challenge — Our Creative Constraints

This is the heart of IMIN. We don't just follow rules — we embrace constraints as a **creative discipline**.

### The Three Prohibitions:

| Constraint | Rationale |
|------------|-----------|
| 🚫 **No Face** | Protects awrah, eliminates riya, centres the message over the messenger. Also protects creators from unwanted public exposure. |
| 🚫 **No Hands** | Extends the faceless principle. Forces creators to think in motion graphics, typography, screen recordings, nature footage, whiteboard animation, voice-over visuals, and AI-assisted imagery. |
| 🚫 **No Music** | Aligns with the majority scholarly opinion on musical instruments. We use nasheed (vocals only), natural soundscapes, ambient nature audio, Quranic recitation, spoken word, and silence as creative tools. |

### What This Unlocks:

- ✍️ Kinetic typography & lyric videos
- 🎙️ Powerful voice-over narration
- 🖥️ Screen-capture tutorials & walkthroughs
- 🎨 Motion graphics & 2D/3D animation
- 📸 Nature cinematography & B-roll storytelling
- 📖 Whiteboard / hand-drawn explainer (digitally simulated)
- 🤖 Ethical AI-assisted visual generation
- 🕌 Quran recitation overlays with transliteration
- 📊 Data visualisation & infographic videos
- 🗣️ Podcast-style audio with visual accompaniment

> *"And whoever fears Allah — He will make for him a way out. And will provide for him from where he does not expect."*
> — Surah At-Talaq (65:2-3)

The constraint is not a cage. It is a doorway to innovation.

## 6. Ethical Business Charter

IMIN operates on a strict ethical business framework derived from Islamic commercial jurisprudence (Fiqh al-Mu'amalat).

### 6.1 Prohibited (Haram) Practices:

| Practice | Islamic Basis |
|----------|---------------|
| Riba (interest-based deals) | Surah Al-Baqarah (2:275) |
| Gharar (excessive uncertainty/deception) | Sahih Muslim (1513) |
| Promoting haram products (alcohol, gambling, etc.) | Surah Al-Ma'idah (5:2) |
| False advertising / misleading claims | Surah Al-Mutaffifin (83:1-3) |
| Exploitative labour practices | *"Pay the worker his wages before his sweat dries."* — Ibn Majah (2443) |
| Plagiarism / IP theft | Violation of Amanah (Surah An-Nisa 4:58) |

### 6.2 Encouraged (Halal) Practices:

- Transparent sponsorship disclosures (#ad, #sponsored)
- Profit-sharing models (Mudarabah / Musharakah)
- Fair-trade and eco-conscious brand partnerships
- Community-funded projects (Waqf model for content)
- Skill-sharing and free knowledge dissemination
- Supporting fellow IMIN members' ventures

### 6.3 Sponsorship Vetting:

All brand partnerships brought into the IMIN workspace must pass the **Halal Compliance Checklist**:

- [ ] Product/service is halal
- [ ] No interest-based financial products
- [ ] No promotion of immodesty
- [ ] Transparent contract terms
- [ ] Fair compensation (no exploitation of small creators)
- [ ] Brand does not engage in Islamophobic practices
- [ ] Environmental and social responsibility verified

## 7. Content Guidelines — The IMIN Code

All content associated with IMIN (personal channels of members, collaborative projects, directory listings) must adhere to:

### 7.1 Visual Rules:
- No human faces shown
- No human hands shown
- No animate depictions that violate scholarly guidelines
- Modesty in all visual representation
- No inappropriate imagery

### 7.2 Audio Rules:
- No musical instruments
- Vocals/nasheed permitted (scholarly difference noted — we default to cautious)
- Natural sounds, ambient audio, recitation encouraged
- Sound effects (non-musical) permitted

### 7.3 Content Integrity:
- No misinformation
- No sectarian hatred or takfir
- No political extremism
- Fact-checked Islamic references (cite scholars/sources)
- Respectful representation of all madhabs within Ahlus Sunnah wal Jamaah
- Sensitivity towards other faiths — no mockery, no hate

### 7.4 Language:
- No profanity
- No vulgar humour
- Urdu, Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Bengali, Gujarati, Marathi, Kannada, Arabic — all welcome
- Multilingual content encouraged (Indian Muslim diversity is our strength)

### 9.1 Directory Entry Includes:

```yaml

  - instagram: "handle"
  - podcast: "link"

location: "City, State (optional)"

skills_needed: ["Animation", "Thumbnail Design"]
availability: "Open for collab | Busy | Mentoring only"
joined: "2026-08-06"
niyyah: "One sentence — why do you create?"
```

### 9.2 Directory Rules:
- Verified by 2 existing members before listing
- Updated quarterly
- Inactive for 6 months → moved to archive
- No real names required (kunya/alias encouraged)
- No follower counts displayed (we reject metric-worship)

## 11. How to Join IMIN

### Step-by-Step:

1. **Read** this README in full. Understand the constraints, ethics, and spirit.
2. **Check** the [Code of Conduct](#14-code-of-conduct--adab-al-muamala). If it resonates, proceed.
3. **Fork** this repository.
4. **Create** your directory entry using the template in `/templates/member-entry.yaml`.
5. **Submit** a Pull Request with your entry.
6. **Wait** for 2 existing members to verify your application.
7. **Introduce** yourself in the "Friday Introductions" thread.
8. **Begin** contributing — ideate, collaborate, create.

### Requirements:
- You must be an Indian Muslim creator (or aspiring one)
- You must agree to the faceless, music-free content guidelines
- You must agree to the Ethical Business Charter
- You must state your niyyah (intention) in your application
- You must be 16+ years of age

> *"And whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it."*
> — Surah Az-Zalzalah (99:7)

Even the smallest contribution matters here.

> — Surah Al-Baqarah (2:195)

### PR Guidelines:

1. Fork → Branch (`feature/your-name-description`) → Commit → PR
2. Follow the YAML/Markdown templates exactly
3. One logical change per PR
4. Be patient — reviewers are volunteers doing this as sadaqah
5. Respond to feedback with adab (courtesy)
6. No PRs that violate the Content Guidelines or Ethical Charter

### Commit Message Convention:

```

types: add, fix, update, docs, asset, refactor
scope: directory, workspace, docs, scripts, assets
```

Example: `add(directory): new member entry — Abu Yusuf, motion graphics`

## 14. Code of Conduct — Adab al-Mu'amala

*Adab al-Mu'amala: The Etiquette of Dealings*

### 14.1 Core Principles:

1. **Respect (Ihtiram):** Treat every member with dignity regardless of skill level, sect, region, gender, or background.
2. **Honesty (Sidq):** No misrepresentation. No fake engagement. No plagiarism.
3. **Patience (Sabr):** Give people time. Not everyone works at your pace.
4. **Humility (Tawadu):** No one is above feedback. No one is below teaching.
5. **Privacy (Satr):** Do not expose another member's faults. Cover them as you'd want Allah to cover yours.
6. **Accountability (Muhasaba):** Own your mistakes. Apologise sincerely. Correct course.
7. **No Backbiting (Gheebah):** Discuss issues directly or through mediators. Never behind backs.

> *"O you who believe! Avoid much suspicion; indeed, some suspicion is sin. And do not spy or backbite each other."*
> — Surah Al-Hujurat (49:12)

### 14.2 Enforcement:

- First violation → Private reminder with nasihah (counsel)
- Second violation → Public warning (without naming, where possible)
- Third violation → Temporary suspension
- Severe/ repeated violation → Permanent removal from directory & workspace
- Appeals handled by Shura council

### 14.3 Zero Tolerance:

- Islamophobia (internalised or external)
- Sectarian hatred / takfir
- Caste discrimination
- Sexual harassment
- Fraud / scam promotion
- Deliberate community sabotage

## 17. Governance & Shura

> *"And whose affair is [determined by] consultation among themselves."*
> — Surah Ash-Shura (42:38)

IMIN is governed by **Shura** (mutual consultation):

- **Shura Council:** 5–7 elected members serving 1-year terms
- **Decisions:** Major changes (charter amendments, tooling shifts, partnerships) require community vote
- **Transparency:** All council minutes published (with sensitive matters redacted)
- **Elections:** Annual, open to all active members
- **Dispute Resolution:** Mediation first → Shura council ruling → Community vote for appeals

No single owner. No dictator. This is a collective amanah.

## 19. FAQ

**Q: Do I have to be a "big" influencer to join?**
A: Absolutely not. If you have 10 followers or 100,000 — you are welcome. We reject follower-count hierarchy. Your niyyah and ethics matter more than your metrics.

**Q: What if I disagree with a scholarly ruling on music/images?**
A: IMIN follows a cautious default (no music, no face/hands). We respect scholarly diversity but maintain this standard for consistency. You are free to follow your madhab personally — IMIN's content standard is simply our collective agreement.

**Q: Can non-Muslims contribute?**
A: The directory and workspace are for Indian Muslim creators. However, open-source code contributions (scripts, website, tooling) are welcome from anyone who respects our mission. Allies are valued.

**Q: What about women creators?**
A: Women are equally welcomed, encouraged, and centred. The faceless format additionally provides a layer of privacy and safety. Gender-segregated collaboration spaces are available if preferred.

**Q: Is this only for Sunni Muslims?**
A: IMIN is anchored in Ahlus Sunnah wal Jamaah broadly. We welcome all Sunni schools (Hanafi, Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali) and do not engage in sectarian gatekeeping. We ask that all members maintain mutual respect.

**Q: Can I use AI tools for content?**
A: Yes, with ethical guidelines. AI-generated imagery must not depict animate beings in prohibited ways. AI-assisted editing, scripting, and design are encouraged. Transparency about AI use is appreciated.

**Q: How is this funded?**
A: Currently volunteer-driven. Future funding through community waqf contributions, ethical sponsorships, and grants. No interest-based funding. Ever.
